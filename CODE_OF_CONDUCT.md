# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect in Phonolite repositories and community spaces. Criticism of code and design is welcome. Keep discussion relevant and constructive, and respect other people's boundaries and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and sharing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [akuremek@telolith.com](mailto:akuremek@telolith.com). Include links or other context where possible, and avoid sharing private details publicly. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to address misconduct.
